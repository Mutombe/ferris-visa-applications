About these numbers

Where these times come from

Published embassy guidance tends to describe the best case. These figures are the median across the files we closed, which is a more useful number when you are booking flights.

  • Median rather than minimum

    Half of our files land faster than the figure shown and half land slower.

  • Measured from submission

    Not from your first enquiry. Add our 48 hour to 7 day preparation on top.

  • Peak seasons run longer

    June to August and December add roughly 30% to most European routes.

  • We quote your case specifically

    Your nationality and route can move the number in either direction, and we will tell you which way.
