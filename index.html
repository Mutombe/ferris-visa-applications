How it works

How your application works

You send your documents once. We complete the forms, book your biometrics appointment and submit the application. You can check where it has got to at any point.

01

Free assessment

Tell us where you are going and why. We confirm which visa applies and whether you are likely to qualify, before you pay anything.

02

Document checklist

You get a checklist written for your case. Upload the documents securely and we will chase whatever is missing.

03

Forms & booking

We complete the official forms, check every answer twice and book your biometrics or interview appointment.

04

Submission & tracking

We submit the application and track it through to a decision. If the embassy queries anything we respond the same working day.

Refused because of our mistake? We rebuild the application and refile at no charge.
Why Ferris

Where most applications fail

Most refusals have nothing to do with whether you qualify. They come from a date that does not match your other documents, a payment into your account that nobody explained, or a form field answered the wrong way round. Those are the things we check for.

  • A named case handler

    One person handles your file from start to finish, and you have their direct line.

  • Fixed fees, quoted upfront

    Our service fee and the embassy fee, both in writing before we start. We do not bill by the hour.

  • A second pair of eyes

    Another handler checks the whole file before we submit it.

  • Encrypted document handling

    Passport scans and bank statements sit in an encrypted vault, and we delete them when you ask.
