What every fee covers

There is no premium tier. A $49 tourist file goes through exactly the same process as a $219 settlement case.

  • Eligibility assessment

    Done before you pay, so you know whether applying is worth it.

  • A checklist built for your case

    Written around your nationality, your route and your circumstances.

  • Forms completed and checked

    We fill them in and a second handler goes over every answer.

  • Appointment booking

    Biometrics and interview slots. We also watch for cancellations if you need an earlier date.

  • Tracking to decision

    Weekly status updates, and same day responses to any embassy query.

  • Free refile on our errors

    If a refusal comes back to something we got wrong, we rebuild and resubmit at no charge.

The process

The same four steps, every time

①

Assessment

A short call or a form. We confirm the route and your chances, and quote the full cost including embassy fees.

②

Documents

You upload to the encrypted vault against a checklist written for your case. We chase anything missing.

③

Preparation

We complete the forms, a second handler reviews them, and we book the appointment at the right consulate.

④

Submission

Filed and tracked. You get weekly updates, same day responses to queries, and the decision the hour it reaches us.

✓

After approval

We explain your entry conditions in plain English and remind you before the visa expires.

We read the notice, explain which ground was cited, and tell you whether reapplying is worth it.
